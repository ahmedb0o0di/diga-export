Incoterms 2024: A Comprehensive Buyer's Guide to Choosing Between FOB, CIF, and CFR

DIJA Trade Compliance Desk • 15 Oct, 2024 • 8 Min Read • Global Shipping

In international commodity trade and global ocean shipping, selecting the appropriate International Commercial Terms (Incoterms®) is among the most consequential commercial decisions an importer can make. Published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), Incoterms define the exact point where costs, operational risks, and logistics responsibilities transfer from the exporter to the buyer.

Making the wrong choice can expose your enterprise to unexpected port demurrage penalties, unhedged maritime insurance risks, and costly terminal handling charges (THC). Below, our senior logistics and trade desk outlines the practical considerations when choosing between the three most prevalent ocean freight terms: FOB (Free On Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), and CFR (Cost and Freight).

"In international commodity trade, contractual clarity is the foundation of profitability. Aligning Incoterms with your destination port handling capacity eliminates demurrage and avoids unexpected terminal surcharges."

Trade Logistics Director — DIJA Group Export

Understanding how each Incoterm functions in real-world port operations enables commercial buyers to negotiate with confidence and optimize total landed costs:

FOB (Free On Board): The seller completes delivery once goods are loaded on board the vessel nominated by the buyer. Best for buyers with preferential maritime carrier volume contracts.
CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight): The seller contracts and pays ocean freight and marine insurance to the destination port. Ideal for buyers seeking turnkey cost certainty.
CFR (Cost and Freight): The seller pays transport to the named port, but risk transfers once goods pass the ship's rail at origin. Buyer procures their own marine insurance.
Documentation Integrity: Mandatory preparation of clean on-board Bills of Lading, Commercial Invoices, and Official Certificates of Origin.
Demurrage Prevention: Real-time container telemetry and pre-clearance filing at destination hubs to prevent port congestion fees.

At DIJA Group Export, our export operations desk works collaboratively with every buyer to evaluate their destination port infrastructure, transit timelines, and financing terms. Whether shipping bulk grains, minerals, or temperature-regulated perishables in refrigerated containers, we tailor shipping contracts that ensure on-time delivery with zero hidden surprises.
