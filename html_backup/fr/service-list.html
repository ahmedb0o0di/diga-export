Questions Fréquentes sur l'Exportation

What international Incoterms do you support for export shipments?
We support all major Incoterms 2020 including FOB (Free On Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), CFR, and EXW according to your destination port requirements and maritime freight preferences.
What quality certificates and inspections accompany each shipment?
All export consignments are inspected by accredited third-party surveyors (SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) and accompanied by Official Certificates of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificates, and lab analysis certificates (COA).
